Senior Backend Engineer - Cloud Platform Team
Nimbus Freight Technologies
Location: Remote (US) | Full-time

About the Role

Nimbus Freight Technologies builds the logistics platform that powers routing, tracking, and billing for mid-size freight carriers across North America. We're looking for a Senior Backend Engineer to join our Cloud Platform team and help us scale our services as we onboard larger carriers and expand into new regions.

You'll own core services that process tens of millions of shipment events per day, work closely with product and SRE teams, and mentor mid-level engineers on the team.

What You'll Do

- Design, build, and operate distributed backend services in Python and Go
- Lead migration of legacy monolith components to containerized microservices
- Own service reliability: define SLOs, build dashboards, and participate in an on-call rotation
- Collaborate with data engineering on event streaming pipelines (Kafka)
- Review code, mentor junior and mid-level engineers, and help set technical direction for the team
- Partner with product managers to scope and estimate new features

Required Qualifications

- 5+ years of professional backend software engineering experience
- Strong proficiency in Python or Go in a production environment
- Experience designing and operating RESTful APIs and microservices
- Solid understanding of relational databases (PostgreSQL preferred) and SQL query optimization
- Experience with containerization and orchestration using Docker and Kubernetes
- Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines and infrastructure-as-code (Terraform preferred)
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with experience working on distributed/remote teams

Preferred Qualifications

- Experience with event-driven architectures and message brokers (Kafka, RabbitMQ, or similar)
- Hands-on experience with a major cloud provider (AWS preferred; GCP or Azure acceptable)
- Familiarity with GraphQL API design
- Experience with observability tooling (Prometheus, Grafana, Datadog, or similar)
- Prior experience in logistics, supply chain, or fintech domains
- Contributions to open-source projects

- Comprehensive health, dental, and vision coverage
- 401(k) match

- Flexible PTO
